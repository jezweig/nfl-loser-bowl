📋 The Rules

  • Pick 1 team you expect to LOSE each week.
  • Pick deadline: Sunday at 1:00 PM EST. Picks for Thursday night games must be in before kickoff.
  • No repeat picks — once you've picked a team, you can't pick them again.
  • 2 strikes and you're eliminated. A strike happens when your picked team wins.
  • Buyback: if you're eliminated, you may re-enter once, through Week 8, for $20.

✅ How to Enter Your Weekly Pick

  1. Go to Make Your Pick.
  2. Enter your name. First time? Make up a short PIN — you'll use the same one every week to change or confirm your pick.
  3. Pick the team you expect to LOSE from that week's real matchups.
  4. Hit submit — that's it.

The page only shows the current week's games, so there's nothing to accidentally submit ahead of time. Only you can see your own pick until its deadline passes — after that it's visible to everyone on the standings page.
